\begin{abstract}
\noindent This document synthesizes, audits, and de-risks the architectural redesign of \texttt{lisploop} across \texttt{plan\_v2.md}, \texttt{plan\_v2b.md}, marginalia in \texttt{plan\_v2b\_annotated.pdf}, and primary-source verification of the underlying literature. Two audit passes govern the final specification. \textbf{Pass~1 (\texttt{v2.1} $\to$ \texttt{v2.2})} eliminated unproved workshop stacks and mathematical flaws---including a Co-de~Bruijn slot-order contradiction, a Kraft-inequality violation in Bits-Back symmetry subtraction, overlap double-counting in sheaf residuals, misapplication of \texttt{Parsynt} to I/O synthesis, $2^N$ branch forking on \texttt{:filter}, and dead-branch-nogood erasure under naive $O(1)$ ablation. \textbf{Pass~2 (\texttt{v2.2} $\to$ \texttt{v2.3}; \S\ref{sec:pass2-audit})} enforces strict self-bootstrapping purity, quantitative budget soundness under unwhitelisted grammars, and algorithmic simplicity: it removes hardcoded $t=0$ AC/cancellation laws, drops higher-order \texttt{:tabulate}/\allowbreak\texttt{:loop} VM opcodes and bespoke \texttt{:filter}/Berlekamp--Massey host solvers, fixes five measured search-budget cliffs (the $48$-bit \texttt{:code-eval} horizon wall, Stage~A $2\mathrm{k}$-unit starvation, the $57$-bit divisibility guard ceiling, the $4$-candidate coupled-pool cliff, and the $70\%$ node-preparation tax), and cuts overkill core machinery ($\sim 1,050$ LOC clean core).
\end{abstract}

Across nine design iterations (\texttt{deprecated/prompt.md} through \texttt{plan9.md} and \texttt{batteries-and-reflection.md}), \texttt{lisploop} validated four enduring invariants:\sidenote{See \texttt{codec.lisp:1--221}, \texttt{vm.lisp:1--645}, \texttt{frame.lisp:544--580}, and \texttt{evidence.lisp:1--73}. Stage~0 (\S\ref{sec:experiments}) preserves all four---and all $27$ VM opcodes---unchanged.}
(i)~homoiconic S-expressions priced by a self-delimiting prefix bitstream,
(ii)~an anytime-resumable prepaid virtual machine with zero host \texttt{eval},
(iii)~Galois-sound contravariant predicate pullbacks $B(Z) = A(d(X,Z))$ paired with a $3{:}1$ tentative/fallback reserve schedule, and
(iv)~strict separation between sampled conjectures and certified obligations.

Yet both independent audits (\texttt{plan\_v2.md} and \texttt{plan\_v2b.md}) converged on the same empirical diagnosis at commit \texttt{df33ed1}: \textbf{the \texttt{frame-guards} representation has hit a complexity and self-bootstrapping ceiling.} Out of $17,818$ pure-kernel lines of Common Lisp ($25$ modules; \texttt{cccc} cognitive complexity $7,376$, cyclomatic $7,021$ across $1,638$ functions), roughly $4,000$ lines encode host rule content, $21$ proposal generators, and $355$ lines of hand-authored recursion skeletons (\texttt{bootstrap/recursion-schemas.lisp}, cognitive complexity $311$). Only $2$ of $52$ operational entries in \texttt{library/*.sexp} (\texttt{:map} and \texttt{:foldr}, $3.8\%$) arise from unassisted synthesis plus library anti-unification, and trace-derived reflective entries move warm-search work by $0$ units when ablated (\S\ref{sec:audit}).

Auditing \texttt{plan\_v2.1} and \texttt{v2.2} across mathematical soundness (Pass~1) and bootstrapping purity, search budgets, and computational complexity (Pass~2) yields \textbf{six clean-core rectifications} defining \textbf{Kernel \texttt{v2.3}} (Table~\ref{tab:derisk}, \S\ref{sec:arch}):

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.28em]
  \item \textbf{R1 --- Columnar Store $+$ Law-Activated Open-DAG (Zero $t=0$ Host Algebra):} \texttt{v2.1} fused \texttt{ACSets} (\litref{patterson2022}{Patterson et al.\ 2022}), \texttt{egglog}/\texttt{Free Join} (\litref{zhang2023}{Zhang et al.\ 2023}), \litref{zucker2026}{Zucker's (2026)} thinning union-find, \litref{singher2023}{Colored E-Graphs}, \litref{cheung2026}{Chompy}, \litref{rosin2026}{AC multiset rebuilding}, and \litref{cao2023}{babble} ($\sim 10,000$ LOC), where pairing a Co-de~Bruijn subset mask with left-to-right slot order unsoundly equates $x_0 - x_1$ with $x_1 - x_0$; conversely, \texttt{v2.2} hardcoded \texttt{:+} and \texttt{:*} AC/cancellation laws at DAG construction, smuggling \texttt{algebra.sexp} into host Lisp. Kernel \texttt{v2.3} pairs \textbf{Columnar \texttt{cbase} arrays} with \textbf{injective Slot-Canonical Open-DAG handles \texttt{ref = (cid, slot-map)}} (packed 61-bit \texttt{fixnum} fast path), \textbf{law-activated smart-constructor flags} ($t=0$ empty; AC sorting, unit dropping, and cancellation activate on an operator \emph{only} once certified \texttt{grule} laws attest them), a \textbf{base-scoped pure \texttt{behav-table}}, and one \textbf{batch-incremental $<180$-LOC \texttt{mini-egraph}} per consolidation pass (\S\ref{ans:4}, \S\ref{sec:arch-store}).

  \item \textbf{R2 --- Minimal Structural Summaries $+$ Sparse Millibit Naive Bayes (Deferring Spectral/DSL Bloat):} Standalone VSA (\texttt{v2.0}) fails on composition (\litref{adler2024}{Adler \& Shavit 2024}) and disjunctions (\litref{ren2020}{Ren et al.\ 2020}), \texttt{v2.1}'s int8 SGD router overfits $|L|=100$, and \texttt{v2.2} still baked Berlekamp--Massey/correlation solvers plus a 32-dim SVD eigensolver and query interpreter into core structs against \texttt{prompt4.md:206}. Kernel \texttt{v2.3} keeps only \textbf{$O(N)$ generic structural column summaries} in host code, expresses shift/difference/periodicity recognizers as \textbf{bootstrapped homoiconic \texttt{grule} metric-decoder pairs} gated by an online \textbf{sparse integer-millibit Naive Bayes inverted index} ($<2\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ lookup), and reserves closed-form Laplacian/SVD (\litref{park2025}{Park et al.\ 2025}) and compositional \texttt{(:like/:analogy/:act)} addressing as \textbf{optional Stage-3/Pillar-9 affordances} once $|L|\gg 500$ (\S\ref{ans:2}, \S\ref{sec:arch-geom}).

  \item \textbf{R3 --- Two-Fibration Cover Calculus, Top-8 Witness Mass, \& Sort-Key Alignment:} \texttt{v2.1}'s sheaf residual reversed restriction variance and double-counted overlaps, \litref{oconghaile2022}{\'O~Conghaile's (2022)} $\mathbb{Z}$-module $\check{H}^1$ neither decides discrete CSPs nor emits nogoods, and subtracting $-\log_2|\mathrm{Sym}|$ from $\mathrm{bits}(g)$ violates Kraft's inequality ($\mathrm{DL}_{\mathrm{net}} \to -\infty$). Kernel \texttt{v2.3} unifies five cover/chart geometries via $\Sigma_u \dashv \Delta_u \dashv \Pi_u$ $+$ $d^*$, prices overlaps once via a \textbf{partition-of-unity selector}, replaces $O(n!)$ permutation search with \textbf{$O(n\log n)$ synthesized sort keys}, bounds Stage~A witness-set log-sum-exp to the \textbf{$K_{\max}=8$ nearest witnesses} in $O(\log|S_\omega|)$, and eliminates $k!$ partition gauge via \textbf{first-appearance coding} (\S\ref{ans:3}, \S\ref{ans:6}, \S\ref{ans:9}).

  \item \textbf{R4 --- Persistent Difference Facts, $O(|w|)$ Forward-Replay Solver Guards, \& Scoped Proofs:} Unary class intervals (\texttt{plan\_v2b} \S7.6) cannot relate $i$ to $\mathrm{length}(xs)$, while allocating a $144$-word DBM matrix on every enumerated node (\texttt{v2.2}) wastes $10$--$30\times$ hot-path work and Plan~9's blind separator search failed on the $57$-bit integer divisibility guard at $60\mathrm{M}$ work. Kernel \texttt{v2.3} extends \texttt{ast.lisp:718}'s persistent \texttt{facts} list with \textbf{symbolic difference triples \texttt{(:diff u v k)}} ($0$ allocation on unguarded nodes, $\sim 35$ LOC), keeps host totality strictly to primitive domains while importing output-range bounds (e.g.\ \texttt{:mod}) from certified \texttt{grule}s, \textbf{derives exact conditional-solver guards in $O(|w|)$ with zero search via totalized forward replay \texttt{(if G\_def(w) (= (op k w) c) nil)}}, and adds $\{+,-,\times\}$ $\mathbb{Z}[\vec{x}]$ PIT plus 1-step structural induction (\S\ref{ans:10}, \S\ref{ans:14}).

  \item \textbf{R5 --- Zero New VM Opcodes, Generic Memoized \texttt{:necessary} Unfold, \& Parameter-Varying Recursion:} \texttt{v2.1} misapplied \litref{farzan2017}{Parsynt} (which requires a known source loop $f$) and forked $2^{|xs|}$ Colored worlds on \texttt{:filter}, while \texttt{v2.2} smuggled \texttt{map-indexed}/\texttt{foldl} into host Lisp via higher-order \texttt{:tabulate}/\allowbreak\texttt{:loop} opcodes and a \texttt{:filter}-specific propagator. Kernel \texttt{v2.3} \textbf{keeps the 27-primitive VM untouched}: index lenses lower via \texttt{:gather} and synthesized \texttt{(map (positions xs 0) ...)}, selective combinators (\texttt{:filter} and any future branching recursion) decouple in $\le (|X|+1)(|Y|+1)$ states via a \textbf{2-rule generic fix to \texttt{fold-if} (\texttt{(if C 'nil 'nil) -> 'nil} under \texttt{:necessary} $+$ lazy DAG-memoized unfolding)} with zero \texttt{:filter} host code, and T4 sequential tasks synthesize via \textbf{general parameter-varying self-calls $+$ DIP tuple-state promotion $+$ CEGAR replay} (\S\ref{ans:5b}, \S\ref{sec:arch-covers}).

  \item \textbf{R6 --- Bottom-Up Single-Op Column Eval, Sound Horizons, Outcome Hash-Join, \& 4-Bucket Attribution:} Top-down VM evaluation in Stage~A burns $328\mathrm{k}$--$828\mathrm{k}$ work by $24$ bits ($>2\mathrm{k}$ budget before $12$ bits), \texttt{:code-eval}'s $38$-bit type header pushes depth-1 \texttt{foldl} steps to $48$ body bits (past Stage~A's $18$-bit limit and after $>10^7$ arithmetic trees), eager preparation consumes $70\%$ of job work, and \texttt{*max-coupled-slot-candidates* = 4} drops latent-state helpers while $O(D^2)$ AC-3 costs $10^6$ evals at $D=1,000$. Kernel \texttt{v2.3} evaluates enumerated nodes \textbf{bottom-up in one columnar op ($O(|B|)$, $43\times$--$110\times$ less work)}, schedules enumeration by \textbf{effective step cost $\tilde{c}(op)\le 8$} (scoring by exact \texttt{codec.lisp} bits), uses \textbf{two-phase lazy node preparation}, joins coupled expression pools in \textbf{$O(|D_1|+|D_2|)$ via outcome hash-join} (reserving 61-bit bitset AC-3 strictly for value domains $|V|\le 61$), and runs \textbf{4-Bucket Causal Attribution} (\S\ref{ans:1a}, \S\ref{ans:15}, \S\ref{sec:pass2-audit}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Index Frames, Tuple Slicing, and Recognizer Bottlenecks}
\label{sec:audit-index}

In container / Naperian functor semantics (\litref{abbott2005}{Abbott et al.\ 2005}; \litref{gibbons2017}{Gibbons 2017}; \litref{paszke2021}{Paszke et al.\ 2021}), a \texttt{:tuple} of arity $k$ has index set $\mathrm{Fin}(k)$ and a \texttt{:seq} of length $n$ has index set $\mathrm{Fin}(n)$. Using \texttt{:gather} (opcode $\#6$ at \texttt{ast.lisp:125}), pulling back \texttt{(= (gather 'x Z) '(y\_0 ... y\_\{m-1\}))} splits into shape \texttt{(= (length Z) 'm)} plus coordinate memberships $Z[j] \in \{i \in \mathrm{Fin}(n) \mid x_i = y_j\}$---identical to \texttt{fold-equal-tuple}. Six host barriers blocked this in \texttt{v1}: \texttt{lift-frame} hides the call ordinal in \texttt{row-key}, \texttt{operator-step-total-p} (\texttt{ast.lisp:870,882}) vetoes variable-index \texttt{:at} and all \texttt{:gather}, \texttt{unfoldable-parts-p} (\texttt{requirements.lisp:136}) blocks splitting on non-total children, \texttt{tuple-slot-term} rejects \texttt{:seq}, \texttt{:gather} lacks fold rules, and \texttt{proposals.lisp:196} never binds combinators to generated index sequences.\footnote{Container/index lifting separates \textbf{T0} index lenses (\texttt{reverse}, \texttt{rotate}, \texttt{transpose}), \textbf{T1} applicative stencils (\texttt{map}, \texttt{positions}, \texttt{map-adjacent}, \texttt{shift-seq}), \textbf{T2} selective subsequences (\texttt{filter}), and \textbf{T3} fully observed scans ($y_t = f(y_{t-1}, x_t)$, where $Y[t-1]$ is a causal lag column) from \textbf{T4} hidden-state sequential coalgebras (\texttt{foldl} from terminal scalars, \texttt{scan-proj0}; \S\ref{ans:5b}).} Meanwhile, the sole correlation recognizer (\texttt{:solve-shift-seq-by-correlation} in \texttt{sequence-shift.sexp}) has no trigger, so \texttt{condense.lisp:236} invokes $O(N^2)$ VM correlation twice on every candidate and missed row ($3,334$--$3,637$ work units per non-shift miss against a $2,000$-unit Stage~A budget), while unnormalized $\sum x_i y_i$ fails on negative inputs, distractors, and affine shifts $a x_{t-k} + b$.

\ansback{q:1}{Prompt Q1} \ansback{q:1a}{Prompt Q1a} \ansback{q:m7}{Margin M7}
\textbf{Yes---$100\%$ compatible without relaxing homoiconicity.} Today, \texttt{expression-closure} (\texttt{vm.lisp:439--551}) allocates AOT-compiled host closures per AST node ($4.0\times$--$7.5\times$ speedup in \texttt{plan-exec.md}), but never invokes SBCL's native x86-64 compiler, unpacks boxed argument lists via \texttt{nth}, runs recursive calls without fuel preemption before \texttt{:settle}, and declines defined functions and \texttt{:code-*} operators.

Margin \hyperlink{q:m7}{\textbf{M7}} (\litref{hoover2026}{CRAM, Hoover et al.\ 2026}) highlights the key principle: search succeeds when exact structure keeps the state space compact \emph{and} per-evaluation latency approaches hardware speed.\sidenote{Audit on lowering \& joins: (i)~SBCL's \texttt{(cl:compile nil ...)} takes $0.5$--$3.0\,\mathrm{ms}$, losing to $2.1\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ closures at $10^3$ evals; (ii)~unboxed \texttt{fixnum} arrays overflow on \texttt{:int} bignums (\texttt{quartic 77}, 128-bit M4); (iii)~top-down step-VM replay in Stage~A re-walks whole trees ($828\mathrm{k}$ work by $24$ bits on $6$ rows); and (iv)~running binary AC-3 over $D=1,000$ expression candidates costs $O(D^2)=10^6$ evals.} Kernel \texttt{v2.3} uses a bignum-safe \textbf{four-tier lowering schedule} (\S\ref{sec:arch-lowering}) where every program remains a canonical S-expression / Open-DAG node:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.15em]
  \item \textbf{Tier~0 (Step VM, $1$--$4$ evals):} Unit-resumable interpreter for cold probes and symbolic CEGAR tracing.
  \item \textbf{Tier~1 (Fuel-Checked Closure Tree, $>4$ scalar evals):} \texttt{expression-closure} ($<5\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ build), extended with inline fuel decrements across defined and recursive calls.
  \item \textbf{Tier~1.5 (Bottom-Up Single-Op Columnar Closures, Stage~A / observation sweeps):} $27$ AOT-compiled array-loop closures in \texttt{vm.lisp} over \texttt{cbase} columns (unboxed \texttt{fixnum} for \texttt{(:gf p)}/\texttt{:bool}; tagged fixnum-with-bignum-fallback for \texttt{:int}). Crucially, bottom-up enumeration evaluates \emph{only the root operator} of \texttt{(op c1 c2)} over already-cached child columns ($O(|B|)$ per node: $7,524$ primitive ops vs.\ $828,242$ top-down VM units up to $24$ bits).
