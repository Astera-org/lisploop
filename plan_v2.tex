\begin{abstract}
\noindent This document synthesizes, rectifies, and extends the architectural audit of \texttt{lisploop} (\texttt{plan\_v2.md}) in light of an independent conservative counter-proposal (\texttt{plan\_v2b.md}), marginalia recorded in \texttt{plan\_v2b\_annotated.pdf}, and primary-source verification of forty-five papers across categorical databases, equality saturation, array/loop synthesis, pseudometric sheaf theory, algorithmic information geometry, computational neuroscience, and reflective programming languages. Where \texttt{plan\_v2.0} over-reached---notably in asking a flat $D=256$ Vector Symbolic Architecture (VSA) to carry deep compositional structure and in framing local geometry as learned vector-space cellular sheaves---we rectify the design using \emph{scaffolded memory theory} (Vector-HaSH / CSCG), \emph{Robinson's sheaves of pseudometric spaces}, and \emph{exact discrete MDL transport with Bits-Back symmetry refunds}. Conversely, where \texttt{plan\_v2b} is overly conservative, incomplete, or subtly flawed---including its broken oracle-placeholder lift on branching combinators, unary totality intervals that cannot relate two variables, sequence-plate bias over general fiber covers, cold-only non-compositional geometry, and host-only ablation---we supply exact mathematical and algorithmic remedies while preserving \texttt{lisploop}'s homoiconic S-expression primacy and scheduled three-tier lowering to native machine code.
\end{abstract}

\section*{Executive Thesis and Rectification Ledger (\texttt{v2.0} $\to$ \texttt{v2.1})}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{Executive Thesis and Rectification Ledger (\texttt{v2.0} \texorpdfstring{$\to$}{->} \texttt{v2.1})}

Across nine design iterations (\texttt{deprecated/prompt.md} through \texttt{plan9.md} and \texttt{batteries-and-reflection.md}), \texttt{lisploop} validated four enduring invariants:\sidenote{See \texttt{codec.lisp:1--221}, \texttt{vm.lisp:1--645}, \texttt{frame.lisp:544--580}, and \texttt{evidence.lisp:1--73}. These invariants survive intact in both \texttt{plan\_v2} and \texttt{plan\_v2b}.}
(i)~homoiconic S-expressions priced by a self-delimiting prefix bitstream,
(ii)~an exact, anytime-resumable prepaid virtual machine with zero host \texttt{eval},
(iii)~Galois-sound contravariant predicate pullbacks $B(Z) = A(d(X,Z))$ paired with a $3{:}1$ tentative/fallback reserve schedule, and
(iv)~strict separation between sampled conjectures and certified obligations.

Yet both independent audits (\texttt{plan\_v2.md} and \texttt{plan\_v2b.md}) converged on the same sobering empirical diagnosis at commit \texttt{df33ed1}: \textbf{the \texttt{frame-guards} representation has hit a complexity and self-bootstrapping wall.} Out of $17,818$ pure-kernel lines of Common Lisp ($25$ modules; \texttt{cccc} cognitive complexity sum $7,376$, cyclomatic sum $7,021$ across $1,638$ functions), roughly $4,000$ lines encode bespoke host rules, $21$ separate proposal generators, and $355$ lines of hand-authored recursion skeletons (\texttt{bootstrap/recursion-schemas.lisp}, whose main function has cognitive complexity $311$---the highest in the repository). Meanwhile, only $2$ of the $52$ operational entries in \texttt{library/*.sexp} (\texttt{:map} and \texttt{:foldr}, $3.8\%$) arise from unassisted synthesis plus library anti-unification, and trace-derived reflective entries move warm-search work by $0$ units when ablated (\S\ref{sec:audit}).

Comparing \texttt{plan\_v2.0} against \texttt{plan\_v2b} and reading the underlying literature in full forces \textbf{six decisive rectifications} that define \textbf{Kernel \texttt{v2.1}} (\S\ref{sec:arch}):

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.35em]
  \item \textbf{R1 --- Substrate Unification (Columnar ACSet Physical Store $+$ Slotted/Thinning E-Graph Semantics):} \texttt{plan\_v2b} rightly insists on contiguous columnar arrays (\litref{patterson2022}{Patterson et al.\ 2022}) and keeping non-monotone control outside the monotone store, while \texttt{plan\_v2.0} rightly insists on congruence, variable-free slot maps, and ATMS assumption contexts. Kernel \texttt{v2.1} unifies them: the physical representation is a \textbf{Columnar Attributed $\mathsf{C}$-Set} (making correlation, difference, and outcome-signature sweeps cache-linear SIMD operations), extended with \textbf{Zucker's 64-bit Co-de~Bruijn thinning bitvectors} (\litref{zucker2026}{Zucker 2026}), \textbf{Colored/ATMS assumption layers} (\litref{singher2023}{Singher \& Itzhaky 2023}), and \textbf{AC multiset e-nodes} (\litref{rosin2026}{Rosin et al.\ 2026}) (\S\ref{ans:4}, \S\ref{sec:arch-store}).

  \item \textbf{R2 --- Geometry Rectification (Demoting Standalone VSA; Adopting Scaffolded Dual-Regime Geometry):} Standalone VSA superposition (\texttt{plan\_v2.0} \S5.2) is refuted as a deep reasoning substrate by \litref{adler2024}{Adler \& Shavit (2024)} (computing $m'$ pairwise features in superposition requires $\Omega(\sqrt{m'\log m'})$ width vs.\ $O(\log m')$ for storage) and \litref{ren2020}{Ren et al.\ (2020)} (disjunctions fail in low dimension). Conversely, \texttt{plan\_v2b}'s non-learned estimators ($k$-NN, Naive Bayes log-odds, WL hashes) excel in the cold regime ($<100$ library entries) but provide no compositional vector algebra for warm libraries. Following \textbf{Vector-HaSH} (\litref{chandra2025}{Chandra et al.\ 2025}) and \textbf{CSCG} (\litref{george2021}{George et al.\ 2021}), \texttt{v2.1} pairs an \emph{exact algebraic base/index scaffold} with bit-calibrated Naive Bayes log-odds at cold start and a compact \emph{local-context bilinear/attention subspace router} (\litref{park2025}{Park et al.\ 2025}; \litref{feng2024}{Feng \& Steinhardt 2024}) once trace/library data accumulate (\S\ref{ans:2}, \S\ref{sec:arch-geom}).

  \item \textbf{R3 --- Sheaf \& Residual Rectification (Pseudometric Sheaves $+$ Bits-Back MDL Transport):} We replace \texttt{plan\_v2.0}'s vector-stalk cellular sheaves---where learned restriction matrices rarely beat identity maps (\litref{caralt2026}{Caralt et al.\ 2026})---with \textbf{Robinson's sheaves of pseudometric spaces} (\litref{robinson2020}{Robinson 2020}) and \textbf{relational CSP presheaves} (\litref{oconghaile2022}{\'O~Conghaile 2022}). Each stalk carries \texttt{lisploop}'s discrete MDL correction pseudometric, transported across arbitrary covers and charts (correcting \texttt{plan\_v2b}'s 1D sequence-plate bias) via three exact laws: zero-Jacobian discrete bijections (\litref{hoogeboom2019}{Hoogeboom et al.\ 2019}), conditional fiber codes, and $-\log_2|\mathrm{Sym}|$ \textbf{Bits-Back symmetry refunds} (\litref{townsend2019}{Townsend et al.\ 2019}). Positive consistency radius $\inf_a \varepsilon(a) > 0$ triggers clause/nogood learning (\S\ref{ans:3}, \S\ref{ans:9}, \S\ref{sec:arch-covers}).

  \item \textbf{R4 --- Relational Difference-Bound Domains \& Inductive Proof Rules:} Contra \texttt{plan\_v2b} \S7.6, unary per-class intervals \emph{cannot} express $0 \le i < \mathrm{length}(xs)$ because $i$ and $\mathrm{length}(xs)$ inhabit distinct classes. Kernel \texttt{v2.1} equips contexts with a two-variable \textbf{Difference-Bound Matrix (Zone) domain} ($x - y \le c$) and breaks the finite-exhaustion ceiling with two sound infinite-domain proof rules: \textbf{structural/Noetherian induction} (\litref{singher2021}{Singher \& Itzhaky 2021}) and \textbf{degree-bounded polynomial/homomorphism identity testing} (\S\ref{ans:10}, \S\ref{ans:14}, \S\ref{sec:arch-proofs}).

  \item \textbf{R5 --- Accumulator Lifting, Angelic Recursion, \& Branch-Safe Recording:} Beyond container lenses (\texttt{tabulate}/\texttt{gather}), \texttt{v2.1} imports \textbf{auxiliary accumulator synthesis} (\litref{farzan2017}{Farzan \& Nicolet 2017}) to lift non-homomorphic sequential loops (\texttt{foldl}, \texttt{scan-proj0}), \textbf{angelic execution with specification strengthening} (\litref{miltner2022}{Miltner et al.\ 2022}) to synthesize recursive functions from non-trace-complete specs, and fixes \texttt{plan\_v2b}'s recorded-execution lift where branching on a dummy placeholder breaks \texttt{:filter} (\S\ref{ans:5b}, \S\ref{sec:arch-covers}).

  \item \textbf{R6 --- Three-Tier Scheduled Lowering \& Native Homoiconic Experimentation:} Preserving $100\%$ S-expression homoiconicity, \texttt{v2.1} schedules lowering across \textbf{Tier~0} (resumable VM/hypergraph), \textbf{Tier~1} (threaded metered closures, \texttt{vm.lisp:439}), and \textbf{Tier~2} (on-demand SBCL \texttt{cl:compile} over unboxed \texttt{fixnum} columns and fast energy/constraint relaxation inspired by \textbf{CRAM}, \litref{hoover2026}{Hoover et al.\ 2026}), while elevating experimentation---interventional \texttt{do}-probes, active disambiguating queries, and $O(1)$ ATMS assumption-toggle ablation---into object-language primitives (\S\ref{ans:1a}, \S\ref{ans:15}, \S\ref{sec:arch-lowering}).
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Index Frames, Tuple Slicing, Recognizers, and \texttt{frame-guards}}
\label{sec:audit-index}

\paragraph{Why tuple slicing and sequence index frames are one Container pullback.}
In container / Naperian functor semantics (\litref{abbott2005}{Abbott et al.\ 2005}; \litref{gibbons2017}{Gibbons 2017}; \litref{paszke2021}{Paszke et al.\ 2021}), a \texttt{:tuple} of arity $k$ has index set $\mathrm{Fin}(k)$ and a \texttt{:seq} of length $n$ has index set $\mathrm{Fin}(n)$. Using \texttt{:gather} (already opcode $\#6$ at \texttt{ast.lisp:125}!), pulling back \texttt{(= (gather 'x Z) '(y\_0 ... y\_\{m-1\}))} splits into shape \texttt{(= (length Z) 'm)} plus decoupled coordinate memberships $Z[j] \in \{i \in \mathrm{Fin}(n) \mid x_i = y_j\}$---identical to \texttt{fold-equal-tuple}! Six host barriers blocked this in \texttt{v1}: \texttt{lift-frame} hides the call ordinal in \texttt{row-key} (forbidden by availability), \texttt{operator-step-total-p} (\texttt{ast.lisp:870,882}) vetoes variable-index \texttt{:at} and all \texttt{:gather}, \texttt{unfoldable-parts-p} (\texttt{requirements.lisp:136}) blocks tuple/cons splitting on non-total children, \texttt{tuple-slot-term} rejects \texttt{:seq}, \texttt{:gather} has no fold rules, and \texttt{proposals.lisp:196} never proposes generated index sequences.\footnote{Mathematically, container/index lifting cleanly separates \textbf{T0} pure index lenses (\texttt{reverse}, \texttt{rotate}, \texttt{transpose}), \textbf{T1} applicative stencils (\texttt{map}, \texttt{positions}, \texttt{map-adjacent}, zero-padded \texttt{shift-seq}), \textbf{T2} selective injections (\texttt{filter}), and \textbf{T3} fully observed scans / associative homomorphisms ($y_t = f(y_{t-1}, x_t)$, where observed $Y[t-1]$ is a causal lag column!) from \textbf{T4} hidden-state sequential Mealy coalgebras (\texttt{foldl} from terminal scalars alone, \texttt{scan-proj0}), which require auxiliary accumulator synthesis (\S\ref{ans:5b}).}

\paragraph{Why correlation recognizers choked Stage~A.}
Only one recognizer exists (entry \texttt{:solve-shift-seq-by-correlation} in \texttt{sequence-shift.sexp}). Because it has no geometric trigger, \texttt{condense.lisp:236} invokes \texttt{metric-delta} on \emph{every candidate and every missed row} of type \texttt{(:seq :int)}, evaluating $O(N^2)$ VM correlation twice per miss ($3,334$--$3,637$ work units to reject a single non-shift against a $2,000$-unit Stage~A budget!), while unnormalized $\sum x_i y_i$ plus exact zero-pad replay fails on negative inputs, large distractors, and affine shifts $a x_{t-k} + b$.

\paragraph{A. Validated Insights Imported from \texttt{plan\_v2b} into \texttt{v2.1}.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.2em]
  \item \textbf{Keep predicate pullback; replace the unary row (\S1.1, \S6.1):} Distinguishing the \emph{base-migration fibration} ($\Delta, \Sigma, \Pi$ over site maps) from the \emph{predicate refinement fibration} ($d^*$ via substitution, \litref{mellies2015}{Melli\`es \& Zeilberger 2015}) is exact and clarifies why data layout and logical requirements are two coupled fibrations rather than one undifferentiated edge soup.
  \item \textbf{Columnar array layout over pointer graphs (\S7.2):} Representing columns and structure maps as contiguous typed arrays turns autocorrelation, finite differences, Berlekamp--Massey, and behavior-signature checks into $O(N)$ cache-hot loops rather than pointer-chasing VM walks.
  \item \textbf{Monotone store vs.\ external non-monotone controller (\S6.8, \S7.1):} Budgets, $3{:}1$ reserve scheduling, tentative commitment, and archiving are non-monotone and must sit \emph{outside} the store's monotone fixpoint engine (\S\ref{ans:7}).
  \item \textbf{Persistent behavior-class enumeration \& rule-set minimization (\S3.5, \S3.7, \texttt{E2}/\texttt{E3}):} Pruning the $22{:}1$ to $38{:}1$ candidate-to-behavior redundancy during enumeration and minimizing discovered laws via derivability (\litref{nandi2021}{Ruler}; \litref{cheung2026}{Chompy}) directly eliminates the $1,753$-law closure explosion.
  \item \textbf{Three exact discrete residual pricing laws \& cold-start Naive Bayes log-odds (\S6.5, \S6.7):} Zero-Jacobian discrete bijections, conditional fiber codes, Bits-Back $-\log_2|\mathrm{Sym}|$ refunds, and log-odds Naive Bayes weights measured natively in bits are immediate upgrades over \texttt{v2.0}'s heuristic scaling factor $J_{\mathrm{scale}}$.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{B. Five Technical Bugs and Architectural Holes in \texttt{plan\_v2b}.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.25em]
  \item \textbf{Bug 1 --- ``Lift by recorded execution'' (\texttt{plan\_v2b} \S7.3, line~508) breaks on data-dependent control flow:} \texttt{plan\_v2b} claims symbolic folding in \texttt{requirements.lisp} can be replaced by running the decoder with a logging oracle that returns an opaque placeholder token. \emph{Test this on \texttt{:filter}:} \texttt{(:if (:code-eval Z elem) (:cons elem rec) rec)}. In Common Lisp / \texttt{vm.lisp}, any non-\texttt{nil} placeholder evaluates \texttt{:if} as \textbf{truthy}, silently taking the \texttt{YES} branch on every element and reproducing the exact \texttt{flatten-lifted-conjuncts} hallucination bug (\texttt{frame.lisp:1033})! And if \texttt{Z} controls recursion termination (\texttt{:fix-seq} or guarded search), dummy execution diverges. Recorded execution works \emph{only} for parametric/applicative combinators (\texttt{:map}, \texttt{:foldr} with unbranched cons); selective/branching combinators require \textbf{multi-path symbolic/angelic execution} (\litref{miltner2022}{Burst}) or \texttt{Colored E-Graph} branch contexts!
  \item \textbf{Bug 2 --- Per-e-class unary facts (\texttt{plan\_v2b} \S7.6, line~573) cannot express $0 \le i < \mathrm{length}(xs)$:} Attaching unary intervals $[l, u]$ to single e-classes only bounds variables against \emph{constants}. Relating variable $i$ to $\mathrm{length}(xs)$ requires a \textbf{binary relational domain} (Difference-Bound Matrix $x - y \le c$, \S\ref{ans:10}).
  \item \textbf{Bug 3 --- 1D sequence-plate myopia (\texttt{plan\_v2b} \S7.4) vs.\ general covers and multi-stage residuals:} \texttt{plan\_v2b} reduces lifting to ``plates'' over sequence positions with \texttt{next}/\texttt{prev}, neglecting overlapping chart covers, quotient spaces, bipartite unordered correspondences, and multi-term residual decomposition where approximate models leave structured non-zero error columns (\S\ref{ans:9}, \S\ref{ans:9a}).
  \item \textbf{Bug 4 --- Unbridged ACSet $\leftrightarrow$ E-Graph chasm (\texttt{plan\_v2b} \S6.1--\S6.2, \S7.2):} Plain \texttt{ACSets} have NP-complete general homomorphism matching (\litref{brown2022}{Brown et al.\ 2022}) and \emph{zero} native union-find congruence or binder $\alpha$-equivalence, while \texttt{Colored E-Graphs} have no base-reindexing maps $\Delta/\Sigma/\Pi$. Saying ``the hypergraph is the semantics; columnar tables are the implementation'' (\S7.2) waves past how congruence, slot/thinning maps, and reindexing actually interact (\S\ref{ans:4}, \S\ref{ans:12}).
  \item \textbf{Bug 5 --- Host-bound ablation (\texttt{plan\_v2b} \texttt{E4}):} Rerunning whole synthesis jobs from scratch in host consolidation to ablate one library entry at a time costs $O(|L| \cdot W_{\mathrm{job}})$ work and leaves experimentation outside the object language (\S\ref{ans:15}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{C. Where \texttt{plan\_v2b} Is Unjustly Pessimistic vs.\ Justifiably Cautious.}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.2em]
  \item \emph{Pessimism 1 (``Matching has lost in every small measured case'', \S3.5, \S9.2):} Conflates an $O(|R|\cdot|T|^2)$ unindexed VM self-interpretation artifact on tiny $<20\mathrm{k}$-work fixtures with intrinsic relational join cost (\S\ref{ans:1b}).

\subsubsection{Q1a \& Margin M7: Compatibility with Dual \{Homoiconic + Deferred Lowering\}}
\label{ans:1a}

\ansback{q:1}{Prompt Q1} \ansback{q:1a}{Prompt Q1a} \ansback{q:m7}{Margin M7}
\textbf{Yes---$100\%$ compatible, without relaxing homoiconicity at all.} In fact, combining a columnar hypergraph store with \textbf{scheduled three-tier lowering} strengthens the dual design far beyond what \texttt{df33ed1} currently achieves.

First, inspect what \texttt{vm.lisp:439--551} and \texttt{plan-exec.md} actually do today:\sidenote{In \texttt{plan-exec.md} (\S1), \texttt{expression-closure} already cut \texttt{quartic 77} batch search from $1.83\,\mathrm{s}$ to $0.45\,\mathrm{s}$ ($4.0\times$) and Probe~5's step evaluation from $15.7\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ to $2.1\,\mu\mathrm{s}$ ($7.5\times$) with \emph{zero} ledger drift across $415.5\mathrm{M}$ work units.}
\texttt{expression-closure} does \emph{not} invoke SBCL's native x86-64 compiler \texttt{(cl:compile nil '(lambda ...))} on fused ASTs. Instead, because \texttt{vm.lisp} itself is AOT-compiled by SBCL at load time, each AST node allocates a small pre-compiled host closure that calls \texttt{funcall} on its child closures, unpacks arguments via \texttt{(nth position args)} on boxed lists, mutates dynamic special \texttt{*closure-work*}, and declines defined functions, recursive bodies, non-literal \texttt{:code-eval}, and all nine \texttt{:code-*} primitives ($3,798$ fallback VM runs in the kernel suite).

Your margin note \hyperlink{q:m7}{\textbf{M7}} citing \textbf{CRAM} (\litref{hoover2026}{Hoover, Blayney \& Whittington, \texttt{arXiv:2611.17309v1}, Nov.\ 2026}) puts its finger on a fundamental principle: \emph{combinatorial search succeeds whenever the effective search space is kept small (by exact topology/symmetries) AND per-evaluation latency is driven down to hardware speed}. CRAM solves $99.996\%$ of Sudoku-Extreme ($42\,\mathrm{ms}$ training, $282\times$ fewer FLOPs than looped Transformers) by replacing heavy recurrent forward passes with direct gradient relaxation over a ultra-fast pairwise Hopfield--Tank free energy $E_\theta(p) = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{i,j} p_i^\top K_{ij} p_j + \sum_i B_i^\top p_i$.

In Kernel \texttt{v2.1}, \textbf{every program, rule, guard, and recognizer remains canonically a homoiconic S-expression / e-class} (inspectable by anti-unification, reorientation, and bi-abduction), while execution lowers \emph{strictly on demand} along three tiers (\S\ref{sec:arch-lowering}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.2em]
  \item \textbf{Tier~0 --- Homoiconic Step Interpreter (Cold / Symbolic / One-Off):} Evaluates one AST or open e-node step at a time with exact unit-resumable fuel. Used for cold candidates evaluated on $1$--$2$ discriminating probe sites and for symbolic/angelic execution. Zero compilation overhead.
  \item \textbf{Tier~1 --- Threaded Metered Closure Tree (Warm, $>4$ site evaluations):} \texttt{expression-closure} (\texttt{vm.lisp:439}), extended to inline fuel checks (\texttt{decf *fuel*}) inside recursive and defined-function calls so closures never run unboundedly before \texttt{:settle} (\texttt{vm.lisp:530}). Compilation takes $<5\,\mu\mathrm{s}$.
  \item \textbf{Tier~2 --- Scheduled Native SBCL Lowering (\texttt{cl:compile}) \& Columnar/CRAM Kernels (Hot, $>10^3$ evaluations):} Triggered \emph{only} when an entry's call counter crosses a threshold (accepted library functions, active inner-loop metric decoders, Stage~A batch kernels, or pairwise soft-constraint energies over finite candidate cells). Emits type-declared SBCL lambdas \texttt{(declare (optimize (speed 3) (safety 0)) (type (simple-array fixnum (*)) col-in col-out))} that fuse the entire AST, unbox integers, vectorize across all $M$ base sites in one loop, and support fast CRAM-style discrete/relaxed energy sweeps over coupled fiber cells before exact root verification!
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Q1b: ``Reuse Lost to Matching Cost'' --- Scale vs.\ Implementation}
\label{ans:1b}

\ansback{q:1b}{Prompt Q1b} \ansback{q:m2}{Margin M2}
\textbf{It is $85\%$ an implementation artifact and $15\%$ a scale-threshold effect.} Tracing both figures cited in \texttt{plan\_v2b} \S3.5 back to \texttt{deprecated/results.md} proves this decisively:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.25em]
  \item \textbf{Case~1 (\texttt{results.md:1048--1058}, M3 rewrite rule $a+0=a$):} Plain search cost $182,181$ work; checked-rule search initially cost $2,126,539$ work, and after one-time preparation still cost $740,452$ work (saving $7,732$ evaluation units while paying \textbf{$545,921$ matching/rewriting units}!). \emph{Why?} Because \texttt{simplify-symbolic-expr} scanned an unindexed list of rules at every subterm of all $649$ enumerated trees, and \texttt{:code-less} (\texttt{vm.lisp:186}) serialized both ASTs to prefix bitstreams to compare size---charging a \emph{quadratic} VM tariff per match attempt while still keeping the rewritten duplicates inside the enumeration buckets!
  \item \textbf{Case~2 (\texttt{results.md:1349--1456}, M4 arithmetic propagation):} On $\mathrm{GF}(5)$, child search dropped from $17,763 \to 14,771$ ($-2,992$), while total work rose from $19,338 \to 21,514$ ($+2,176$) because preparing and dispatching $8$ unindexed solver entries cost $\sim 5,100$ fixed overhead on a toy $19\mathrm{k}$-work task. \textbf{Yet on the very next page (\texttt{results.md:1419--1452})}, when forward evaluation context grew to $128$-bit integers, \textbf{exact same propagation cut first-solution work from $2,193,972$ down to $454,323$---a $79.3\%$ net win!}

\subsection{Q3: Sheaf Theory at the Intersection of Topology and Geometry}
\label{ans:3}
% -----------------------------------------------------------------------------

\ansback{q:3}{Prompt Q3}
\textbf{Yes---sheaf theory is the \emph{exact} mathematical bridge between topology (what connects to what) and geometry (local metrics and residuals), and reconciling \texttt{plan\_v2} with \texttt{plan\_v2b} makes it vastly cleaner and more concrete than either plan alone.}

Strip away the abstract jargon and a sheaf has three simple pieces:\sidenote{Intuitively: a \emph{base topology} specifies overlapping local views (rows, sequence windows, decoder fibers); \emph{stalks} hold candidate values/programs over each view; \emph{restriction maps} check whether overlapping choices agree. A \emph{global section} is one unified program that works everywhere.}
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.2em]
  \item \textbf{Base Space / Cover (Topology):} A poset or category $\mathcal{U}$ of local contexts/patches (e.g., individual examples $\omega$, overlapping stencil windows $[t-1, t]$ and $[t, t+1]$, guarded branches $U^+ \cup U^-$, or child subproblems under decoders) with overlap inclusions $U \cap V \hookrightarrow U$.
  \item \textbf{Stalks (Local Spaces):} To each patch $U$, assign the set $\mathcal{F}(U)$ of locally valid values or candidate subprograms over $U$.
  \item \textbf{Restriction / Transport Maps:} Along every inclusion or hyperedge $e : U \to V$, a map $\rho_{U\to V} : \mathcal{F}(U) \to \mathcal{F}(V)$ translates local candidates onto their shared overlap.
\end{enumerate}

Now compare the two flavors of sheaves cited in \texttt{plan\_v2.0} vs.\ \texttt{plan\_v2b}:
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.6em, itemsep=0.25em]
  \item \textbf{Why \texttt{plan\_v2.0}'s Vector Cellular Sheaves (\litref{hansen2019}{Hansen \& Ghrist 2019}; \litref{bodnar2022}{Neural Sheaf Diffusion}) Needed Rectification:} Vector sheaves require every stalk to be $\mathbb{R}^d$ and every restriction map to be a linear matrix $F_{v\trianglelefteq e} \in \mathbb{R}^{d\times d}$. Worse, as \texttt{plan\_v2b} \S6.8 noted and \litref{caralt2026}{Caralt et al.\ (\texttt{arXiv:2603.05395}, 2026)} proved empirically, GNN-learned restriction matrices rarely beat fixing $F_{v\trianglelefteq e} = I$ (the ``Identity Sheaf baseline'')!
  \item \textbf{Why Robinson's Sheaves of Pseudometric Spaces (\litref{robinson2020}{Robinson, \emph{Compositionality} 2020, \texttt{arXiv:1805.08927}}) + CSP Presheaves (\litref{abramsky2011}{Abramsky \& Brandenburger 2011}; \litref{oconghaile2022}{\'O~Conghaile 2022}) Fit \texttt{lisploop} Hand-in-Glove:} Robinson drops linearity completely! Stalks $\mathcal{F}(U)$ are \emph{arbitrary sets}---discrete integers, sequences, or typed program classes---each equipped with a \textbf{pseudometric} $d_U : \mathcal{F}(U) \times \mathcal{F}(U) \to \mathbb{R}_{\ge 0}$ (here: \textbf{MDL correction bits}!), and restriction maps $\rho_{U\to V}$ are actual \emph{verified programs/decoders}! Given an assignment $a = (a_U)_{U \in \mathcal{U}}$ of local candidates (e.g., Stage~A local fits or fiber witnesses), Robinson defines the \textbf{consistency radius}:
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:consistency-radius}
    \varepsilon(a) \;=\; \sup_{U \subseteq V} d_V\!\big(\rho_{U \to V}(a_U),\; a_V\big)
    \quad \text{(or MDL sum }\sum_{e=(U\to V)} d_V(\rho_e(a_U), a_V)\text{)}.
  \end{equation}
  Look at what Eq.~\eqref{eq:consistency-radius} unifies at one stroke:
  \begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*), leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=0.15em]
    \item $\varepsilon(a) = 0$: exact gluing---$a$ is a \textbf{global section} (zero-residual verified program, or conserved invariant in $H^0$)!
    \item $0 < \varepsilon(a) < \infty$ with finite candidate $a$: \textbf{exact Stage~A / chart residual bits}, well-defined across \emph{any} cover or generated space!
    \item Every local stalk $\mathcal{F}(U_i) \ne \varnothing$ ($1$-consistent), yet $\inf_{a} \varepsilon(a) > 0$ (no global section exists): a \textbf{\v{C}ech cohomological obstruction} (\litref{abramsky2011}{Abramsky \& Brandenburger 2011}; \litref{oconghaile2022}{\'O~Conghaile 2022})---which is the \emph{exact mathematical meaning} of a coupled multi-row / multi-slot conflict that unary row propagation cannot see, and whose minimal inconsistent cover support yields a \textbf{CDCL/UIP nogood clause}!
  \end{enumerate}
\end{itemize}

  \item \textbf{No Congruence Closure:} Merging two elements in a $\mathsf{C}$-Set is a colimit/coequalizer, which does \emph{not} propagate functional dependencies $(op, c_1, c_2) \mapsto c_{\mathrm{out}}$ upward to parent e-nodes. \end{enumerate}

\paragraph{3. Answering Margin M1 (\emph{``keep topology nested and exact, keep geometry flat and local, compare across spaces only through verified maps --- how, though?''}):}

(i)~A nested expression or multi-step decoder chain $Y = d_1(X, d_2(X, Z))$ lives as \emph{exact integer foreign-key edges} in the columnar hypergraph (nested topology, zero lossy vector compression).
(ii)~At any single base $B$ and type $\tau$, candidate columns $u, v \in \tau^{|B|}$ are flat vectors of length $|B|$ whose local distance is $d_B(u, v) = \sum_{s \in B} \mathrm{bits}_\tau(u[s], v[s])$ (flat, $O(|B|)$ local geometry).
(iii)~You \emph{never} take raw Euclidean/L1 differences between a child candidate $\hat{z} \in \tau_{\mathrm{child}}^{|P|}$ (say, an index array of length $16$) and a parent target $y \in \tau_{\mathrm{parent}}^{|R|}$ (say, a sequence of integers); instead, you push $\hat{z}$ forward through the verified hyperedge map $d_1 \circ \Delta_u$ (or pull $y$ back via a certified solver $d_1^{-1}$) so both vectors inhabit the \emph{same} typed space before computing distance!

  \item \textbf{Bijective Chart ($d(x, \cdot)$ injective on data, e.g., $Z = Y - \hat{Y}$ or reversible index permutation):} By \textbf{Integer Discrete Flows} (\litref{hoogeboom2019}{Hoogeboom et al., NeurIPS 2019, \texttt{arXiv:1905.07376}}), discrete bijections have \emph{exact unit Jacobian} ($|\det J| = 1$ does not apply; probability masses map $1$-to-$1$), so $\mathrm{DL}(Y\mid X) = \mathrm{bits}(d) + \mathrm{DL}(Z\mid X)$ with \textbf{zero scale distortion}!
  \item \textbf{Surjective / Many-to-One Projection ($Y = \pi(Z)$ or quotient class $c = q(X)$):} By Shannon--Kolmogorov chain decomposition ($K(Z) \approx K(\pi(Z)) + K(Z \mid \pi(Z))$), the residual \emph{is} the conditional index within the fiber $\pi^{-1}(y)$, costing $\log_2 |\pi^{-1}(y)|$ bits under a uniform fiber prior (matching \texttt{plan2.md}'s fiber-entropy formula!).
  \item \textbf{Under-Determined / Gauge-Symmetric Latent ($Z$ defined only up to permutation or multi-witness fiber $S_\omega$):} If $k!$ label permutations of a synthesized partition/quotient yield identical $Y$, or any $z \in S_\omega$ satisfies $d(x_\omega, z) = y_\omega$, naive MDL overcharges the latent model by $\log_2 |\mathrm{Sym}|$ or $\sum_\omega \log_2 |S_\omega|$ bits! By \textbf{Bits-Back Coding} (\litref{townsend2019}{Townsend, Bird \& Barber, ICLR 2019, \texttt{arXiv:1901.04866}}), that exact symmetry entropy must be \textbf{refunded}:
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:bits-back}
    \mathrm{DL}_{\text{net}}(Y \mid X;\, d) \;=\; \mathrm{bits}(d) \;+\; \mathrm{DL}(Z \mid X) \;-\; \log_2 |\mathrm{Sym}(Z \mid X, Y)|.
  \end{equation}
  Without Eq.~\eqref{eq:bits-back}, multi-valued fiber lifts and latent state partitions look artificially worse than flat lookup tables!
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Where \S6.5 hits bounded utility:}

\subsection{Pillar I: Columnar Thinning/Colored Equivalence Store over Base Schemas}

\litentry{balasubramanian1997}{Balasubramanian --- Statistical Inference, Occam's Razor, \& Fisher Geometry}{\emph{Neural Computation} 9(2), 1997; \texttt{arXiv:cond-mat/9601030}; \& \hypertarget{lit:myung2000}{}Myung, Balasubramanian \& Pitt, \emph{PNAS} 2000}{\secback{ans:13}{Q13}}
Interprets MDL model complexity geometrically as the log-volume $\ln \int \sqrt{\det I(\theta)}\,d\theta$ of distinguishable distributions under the Fisher--Rao metric, invariant to reparameterization. Directly grounds \textbf{Q13}: learning verified abstractions and quotienting equivalences reshapes the discrete Fisher sensitivity of the DSL so $1$-edit syntactic neighbors align with meaningful semantic steps!

\section{Literature Review IV: Superposition, Brain Scaffolds, Energy Search, \& Reflection}
\label{sec:app-d}
% =============================================================================

\litentry{adler2024}{Adler \& Shavit --- On the Complexity of Neural Computation in Superposition}{\texttt{arXiv:2409.15318} (2024)}{\secback{sec:thesis}{R2}, \secback{sec:marginalia}{M3}, \secback{ans:2}{Q2}}
Proves the exact theorem answering Margin \hyperlink{q:m3}{\textbf{M3}}: while $m'$ sparse features can be \emph{stored} in $O(\log m')$ neurons (Johnson--Lindenstrauss), \emph{computing} $m'$ pairwise Boolean or permutation features in superposition requires $\Omega(\sqrt{m' \log m'})$ neurons and $\Omega(m' \log m')$ parameters (constructive upper bound $O(\sqrt{m'}\log m')$ neurons), capping $n$-neuron superposed compute at $O(n^2/\log n)$ features! Refutes using flat VSA vectors for multi-step symbolic composition.

\litentry{ren2020}{Ren, Hu \& Leskovec --- Query2box: Reasoning over Knowledge Graphs Using Box Embeddings}{ICLR 2020; \texttt{arXiv:2002.05969}}{\secback{sec:thesis}{R2}, \secback{ans:2}{Q2}}
Proves geometric region intersections work in $O(d)$, whereas representing arbitrary logical \emph{disjunctions} over $N$ entities requires embedding dimension $\Theta(N)$, forcing working systems to keep disjunctions (DNF/contexts) symbolic.

\litentry{frady2020}{Plate (1995) HRR; Kleyko et al.\ (2022, \texttt{arXiv:2111.06077}); \& Frady et al.\ --- Resonator Networks}{\emph{Neural Computation} 32, 2020; \texttt{arXiv:2007.03748}}{\secback{ans:2}{Q2}}
Shows VSA circular-convolution/Hadamard binding preserves inner products locally, but nested unbinding accumulates cross-talk noise scaling as $\sqrt{K/D}$ unless cleaned up at each step against discrete codebooks.

\litentry{peng2024}{Peng, Narayanan \& Papadimitriou --- On Limitations of the Transformer Architecture}{\texttt{arXiv:2402.08164} (2024)}{\secback{ans:2}{Q2}}
Uses communication complexity to prove a single attention layer with $H$ heads and dimension $d$ cannot compose two functions over domain size $N$ unless $H \cdot d = \Omega(N \log N)$.

\litentry{feng2024}{Feng \& Steinhardt --- How do Language Models Bind Entities in Context?}{ICLR 2024; \texttt{arXiv:2310.17191}}{\secback{sec:thesis}{R2}, \secback{ans:2}{Q2}}
Uses causal activation patching to show Transformers bind entities to attributes via low-rank \emph{Binding ID subspaces} orthogonal to attribute content vectors.

\litentry{park2025}{Park, Andrew~Lee, Lubana, Tanaka et al.\ --- ICLR: In-Context Learning of Representations}{ICLR 2025; \texttt{arXiv:2501.00070}}{\secback{sec:thesis}{R2}, \secback{ans:2}{Q2}, \secback{sec:arch-geom}{\S5.3}}
Demonstrates that given random-walk traces on an arbitrary graph in context, Transformer residual representations undergo a sharp phase transition that minimizes \emph{graph Dirichlet energy}, aligning principal components with the graph's \emph{Laplacian spectral embedding}---confirming your intuition that contextual operators dynamically reorganize flat geometric coordinates!

\litentry{whittington2020}{Whittington, Muller, Mark, Chen, Barry, Burgess \& Behrens --- Tolman--Eichenbaum Machine}{\emph{Cell} 183(5), 2020}{\secback{ans:2a}{Q2a}, \secback{ans:2a1}{Q2a.1}}
Gradient-trained BPTT model factorizing abstract transition location $g_t$ (medial entorhinal cortex, updated over a fixed action set) from sensory content $x_t$, bound via Hebbian outer products in hippocampus. Confirms \textbf{Q2a.1}: insightful factorization, but fixed-action gradient BPTT is ill-suited to cold-start synthesis.

\litentry{george2021}{George et al.\ --- Clone-Structured Cognitive Graphs (\emph{Nat.\ Comm.} 2021); \& \hypertarget{lit:raju2024}{}Raju et al.}{\emph{Science Advances} 2024, DOI \texttt{10.1126/sciadv.adm8470}}{\secback{sec:thesis}{R2}, \secback{ans:2a}{Q2a}}
Resolves perceptual aliasing (\texttt{scan-proj0} / DIP conflicts!) without continuous gradients by discretely \emph{cloning} latent states and fitting transition routing via EM, with spatial geometry emerging from the learned transition graph.

\litentry{chandra2025}{Chandra, Sharma, Chaudhuri \& Fiete --- Vector-HaSH (\emph{Nature} 2025, DOI \texttt{10.1038/s41586-024-08392-y}); \& Gershman, Fiete \& Irie}{\emph{Neuron} 2025, \texttt{arXiv:2501.02950}}{\secback{sec:thesis}{R2}, \secback{ans:2a}{Q2a}, \secback{sec:arch-geom}{\S5.3}}
Proves the hippocampus avoids Hopfield's $0.14N$ memory cliff by heteroassociatively binding flat neocortical content vectors onto a \emph{pre-structured algebraic grid-cell scaffold} (a residue number system $\prod \mathbb{Z}_{m_k}$ with exponential error-corrected states). Directly inspires \texttt{v2.1}'s exact columnar scaffold + flat local content vectors!

\litentry{hoover2026}{Hoover, Blayney \& Whittington --- Classical Energies Learn to Reason $1{,}000\times$ Faster (\texttt{CRAM})}{\texttt{arXiv:2611.17309v1}, 12 Nov.\ 2026}{\secback{sec:thesis}{R6}, \secback{sec:marginalia}{M7}, \secback{ans:1a}{Q1a}, \secback{sec:arch-lowering}{\S5.5}}
Modernizes Hopfield--Tank pairwise energy networks ($E_\theta(p) = \frac{1}{2}\sum p_i^\top K_{ij} p_j + \sum B_i^\top p_i$, trained via pseudolikelihood/contrastive energy shaping without BPTT), solving $99.996\%$ of Sudoku-Extreme ($42\,\mathrm{ms}$ training, $282\times$ fewer inference FLOPs than looped Transformers) and $100\%$ of Maze-Unique ($132\,\mathrm{ms}$). Directly validates Margin \hyperlink{q:m7}{\textbf{M7}}: when evaluation per step is lowered to ultra-fast compiled kernels over compact constraint spaces, direct search/energy relaxation crushes heavy recurrent models!

\litentry{kuhlwein2013}{K\"uhlwein et al.\ --- MaSh (ITP 2013); Gauthier et al.\ --- TacticToe (2021); \& \hypertarget{lit:blaauwbroek2024}{}Blaauwbroek et al.}{Graph2Tac, ICML 2024; \& \hypertarget{lit:grand2024}{}Grand et al.\ --- LILO, ICLR 2024, \texttt{arXiv:2310.19791}}{\secback{ans:2a}{Q2a}, \secback{sec:arch-geom}{\S5.3}}
In online, data-scarce theorem proving, sparse Naive Bayes (log-odds weights in bits!) and online $k$-NN (\texttt{MaSh}, \texttt{Graph2Tac}'s $1.72\times$ online $k$-NN gain) rival or beat heavy offline neural models and update in $O(1)$ after every proof, while \texttt{LILO} proves compact compositional names measurably improve downstream synthesis search.

\litentry{smith1984}{Smith --- Reflection and Semantics in Lisp (POPL 1984); \hypertarget{lit:desrivieres1984}{}des~Rivi\`eres \& Smith (LFP 1984 / 1990); \hypertarget{lit:wand1986}{}Wand \& Friedman (1986); \hypertarget{lit:maes1987}{}Maes (OOPSLA 1987); \& \hypertarget{lit:amin2018}{}Amin \& Rompf}{Collapsing Towers of Interpreters, POPL 2018}{\secback{ans:11}{Q11}}
3-Lisp introduced causal procedural reflection via an infinite tower reifying environments and continuations. Retrospectives (\texttt{3-KRS}, \texttt{Black}, \texttt{Collapsing Towers}) established two enduring rules: (1)~reifying raw procedural continuations blocks compilation and creates low-density intensional sprawl, whereas declarative meta-object schemas keep reflection learnable; and (2)~restricting causal reflection to staged boundaries (Amin \& Rompf 2018) allows partial evaluation/compilation to collapse reflective overhead to zero!

\litentry{lenat1984}{Lenat \& Brown --- Why AM and EURISKO Appear to Work}{\emph{Artificial Intelligence} 23(3), 1984, pp.\ 269--294}{\secback{ans:7}{Q7}, \secback{ans:13}{Q13}}
Diagnoses why AM succeeded on elementary math (Lisp list operations matched set-theoretic semantics with high mutation density) while EURISKO stalled when mutating monolithic Lisp heuristics until heuristics were factored into fine-grained typed \texttt{RLL} slots---motivating both typed per-level schemas and library-driven Fisher metric reshaping (\S\ref{ans:13}).

\litentry{goertzel2023}{Goertzel, Bogdanov, Duncan et al.\ --- OpenCog Hyperon \& MeTTa}{\texttt{arXiv:2310.18318} (2023)}{\secback{ans:7}{Q7}}
Places data, rules, and self-modifying metaprograms in one unrestricted AtomSpace metagraph; illustrates the severe matching-cost and soft-truth-compounding hazards when congruence canonicalization, monotone separation, and MDL utility pruning are absent.

\litentry{liao2025}{Liao \& Gu --- ARC-AGI Without Pretraining (\texttt{CompressARC})}{\texttt{arXiv:2512.06104} (2025), 3rd Place ARC Prize 2025}{\secback{sec:alternatives-comparison}{\S3.2}, \secback{ans:7}{Q7}}
Trains a $76\mathrm{K}$-parameter equivariant multitensor residual network from scratch on each single ARC puzzle via Adam to minimize variational Bits-Back KL + reconstruction MDL, solving $20\%$ of ARC-AGI-1 eval ($\sim 20\,\mathrm{min}$ GPU/puzzle) with zero pretraining, but retaining zero cross-puzzle library knowledge and dropping to $\sim 4\%$ on multi-step algorithmic ARC-AGI-2 tasks.
